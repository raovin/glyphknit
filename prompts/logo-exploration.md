# GlyphKnit logo exploration

Design three original black-and-white identities for **GlyphKnit**, an independent engineering contracting company led by Vin Rao. Clients hire GlyphKnit to build test infrastructure, improve release pipelines, and find failures in software and data systems. The mark will appear in a proposal header, beside a drawing number in a technical report, and in a browser tab.

1. **Bespoke wordmark.** Draw “GlyphKnit” with a custom lowercase h and capital K. Explore a shared angle or terminal where those strokes meet. Keep both letters legible and give the rest of the lettering room.
2. **Fitted joint.** Pair a compact symbol with a wordmark. Draw two solid shapes that fit together and leave one distinctive piece of negative space at the join. Test it as a black stamp.
3. **Printer’s mark.** Start from the silhouette of a G. Design an emblem small enough to sit beside a report’s page number. Give it its own typographic direction.

Show each direction on white at the same scale, as a horizontal signature, an actual-size 16 px favicon, and a plain document header reading *Engineering proposal*. Preserve the exact spelling **GlyphKnit**. Omit taglines, gradients, yarn, circuit boards, brackets, shields, and dramatic mockups. Explain the drawing decision in one sentence per concept.

For the second round, refine the strongest idea in black, reversed white, and muted teal. Compare small-size legibility, recognition of the company name, and reproduction on an ordinary printed page.
